Anonymous & Secure — Privacy at keyrefinder.ch

When you lose your keys in Switzerland, your worry isn't just the high cost of replacing the lock — it's the security of your home. What happens if your keys, along with your name or address, end up in the wrong hands?

The keyrefinder.ch key return system was built precisely to eliminate this risk entirely. Every key return we process is 100% anonymous and secure. Here's how we protect the privacy of both owners and finders at the highest level.

How anonymity works — the technical side

The principle behind our system is elegantly simple yet highly effective: our solid stainless steel tags carry no name, no address, and no phone number. The only thing engraved is a unique, randomly assigned identification number (ID) together with a contact URL.

Only our central database, secured in Switzerland, can link this ID number to a name and address. Neither the finder nor any third party can determine who the key owner is or where they live based on the key alone.

What the finder sees (and what they don't)

You've found someone's keys? Your willingness to help should never come at the cost of your own privacy.

  • Post option: When you drop the key unwrapped into a post box, you reveal absolutely nothing about yourself — no name, no return address.
  • Direct contact option: When you use the online form, our system acts as a neutral "trustee". We forward your message to the owner without disclosing your email address or phone number. You can arrange a discreet handover without ever revealing your identity.

What the owner sees (and what they don't)

Have you lost your keys and someone found them? When we contact you to report the find, you won't receive any personal data about the finder either (unless they choose to share it voluntarily during direct contact). There's no need to worry about strangers knowing whose keys they were or where you live. Your home address is used exclusively by us to return your keys by registered post.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ) about privacy

Is my customer data stored in Switzerland at keyrefinder.ch?

Yes. As a Swiss company, we place the highest value on Swiss quality and security standards. Your registration data is managed securely and in full compliance with Switzerland's strict Data Protection Act (revDSG).

Does keyrefinder.ch share data with third parties (such as advertising partners)?

No, absolutely not. The data entrusted to us serves exclusively the purpose of the key return system. Customer care and data security are our hallmarks. We do not sell addresses and do not send unsolicited third-party advertising.

Is the return service free for life?

Maintaining secure data management and a professional Swiss service requires ongoing infrastructure investment. Unrealistic promises like a "free-for-life service" are not part of our business philosophy. The service operates on fair, transparent subscription terms. More details in the general FAQ.

Does the finder find out whose keys they are?

No. The ID number on the tag can only be decoded by the keyrefinder central team. For the finder, the key ring remains completely anonymous — which entirely eliminates any risk of misuse following a loss.
