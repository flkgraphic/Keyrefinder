Materials & Craftsmanship: Swiss Quality for Your Brand

A corporate gift only succeeds when customers actually use it every day. A key ring faces extreme conditions — scratches, impacts and moisture are constant companions. That's precisely why keyrefinder.ch makes no compromises on quality.

We produce exclusive, unique pieces from durable materials that will reflect your brand identity flawlessly for decades. Don't settle for short-lived standard solutions made of plastic.

Premium stainless steel – the foundation of KeyRefinder

Every classic KeyRefinder® and practical KeyShopper® is crafted from high-quality, solid, rust-free stainless steel. This material choice delivers decisive advantages for you and your clients:

1. Extreme durability (sustainability)

Forget disposable giveaways. Plastic breaks, printed aluminium scratches. Our solid stainless steel is virtually indestructible. Your corporate gift will last decades, keeping your brand continuously in your customer's field of view. That's real, lived sustainability — and a reflection of your company's values.

2. Premium feel and weight

Details make the difference in brand perception. Solid stainless steel has a satisfying, high-quality weight in the hand. The moment your customer picks up their keys, they feel the quality — a psychological effect that builds trust in your company.

3. Perfect for the key return system

A key return system only works reliably if the ID number remains readable for years to come. The engraving on our stainless steel doesn't wear down with daily use, so every found key can be traced back to its owner without difficulty.

Noble finishes: polished or matte

Match the look precisely to your corporate identity. Give your products an exclusive touch with the right finish:

  • Clear polished finish: Perfect for brilliant colour depth and an especially elegant, classic appearance.
  • Matte finish: The ideal choice for a contemporary, subtly technical look. Scratches on the matte surface are also practically invisible.

Custom shapes & unlimited branding

We put your brand in its best shape! At keyrefinder.ch, you don't have to adapt your logo to a pre-defined standard form. From a fully bespoke shape of your own logo to high-resolution photo printing — we reproduce your identity with precision. We turn your branding into a tactile experience.

We also round off the perfect first impression with bespoke information leaflets and special packaging. This way, you not only explain the benefit of the anonymous return system to the recipient, but you also have ample space for your individual brand message.
