Found Someone's Keys – What Should You Do? How to Return Them Safely

Found someone's keys and not sure what to do next? Losing your keys is incredibly stressful – so it's wonderful that you want to help! If the key ring you've found has a metal tag from keyrefinder.ch, returning the keys is completely free, absolutely safe, and 100% anonymous for you.

Every day, hundreds of keys are lost across Switzerland. Here's exactly how you can return the keys you found to their rightful owner as quickly as possible using our key return system.

How to return found keys: it's this easy

If the key ring has a KeyRefinder® or KeyShopper tag attached, you have two convenient options:

Option 1: Return by Post (the fastest way)

Simply drop the found key ring — unwrapped — into the nearest yellow Swiss Post letterbox. No stamp, no address needed. Swiss Post automatically forwards the keys to our secure centre. From there, we contact the registered owner and discreetly return their keys. As a finder, your job is done!

Option 2: Direct, anonymous online reporting

On the back of the KeyRefinder® tag you'll find a QR code or a website with an identification number.

  1. Scan the code with your smartphone or enter the number on our website.
  2. Follow the brief on-screen instructions.
  3. Our system creates an anonymous connection between you and the owner. You can arrange a handover without your real phone number or name ever being shared.

Learn more about our Privacy & Anonymity policy.

Why returning keys through keyrefinder.ch is safe and anonymous

As a finder, you want to help — but not at the expense of your privacy. We understand that completely. Our Swiss system was built precisely for this: no exchange of private phone numbers, names, or addresses is needed. Whether you drop the key in a post box or use our online service — your identity remains protected at all times.

What if the key doesn't have a keyrefinder tag?

Found a key without a keyrefinder.ch tag? In that case, please hand the key ring in at your local lost and found office or nearest police station. SBB train stations also have their own lost property offices if you found the key on a train. Please don't drop keys without our tag in a Swiss Post letterbox, as they cannot be matched to their owner.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ) from finders

Does returning the key cost me anything?

No, returning keys is completely free for you as a finder. If you drop the key ring unwrapped into a post box, we or the owner cover the postage.

Do I get a finder's reward?

The KeyRefinder® system is built on the helpfulness and solidarity of the Swiss community. Our system does not pay out an automated finder's fee. However, if you choose direct contact (Option 2), the grateful owner is of course welcome to show their appreciation when you meet.

Can I also hand the key in at a police station?

Yes, that's certainly possible. However, the police will pass the key on to the local lost property office. Dropping the key directly into a post box is significantly faster and involves far less paperwork — our system is designed precisely for this quick route.

What happens to the key after I put it in the letterbox?

Swiss Post sends the key to our secure centre. Using the engraved ID number on the KeyRefinder® tag, we identify the owner in our database, notify them immediately, and return the key by registered post. More questions? Visit the general FAQ.
