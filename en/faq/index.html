Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) about KeyRefinder®

Find the most important answers about Switzerland's leading key return system. Whether you want to use KeyRefinder® as an innovative corporate gift or have questions about security and key return – we have you covered.

Questions for Finders & Key Losers

I found someone's keys – what do I need to do?

Simply drop the found key ring with its KeyRefinder® tag — unwrapped — into the nearest yellow Swiss Post letterbox. Alternatively, you can scan the QR code to report the key anonymously. Find the full step-by-step instructions here: Found a key – what to do?

I've lost my keys – what now?

If your key ring is equipped with a KeyRefinder®, you have an excellent chance of a quick return. Stay calm and check our immediate lost key checklist.

Is my data truly safe and anonymous?

Yes, 100%. Our Swiss system requires no exchange of personal data such as names or phone numbers between the finder and the owner. Learn exactly how we protect your privacy: Privacy & Anonymity.

Questions for Businesses (B2B & Corporate Gifts)

Why is KeyRefinder® such a good corporate gift?

Key rings are the ultimate everyday essential. With a KeyRefinder® as a client gift, your logo is in front of your target audience every single day. It delivers real functional value that builds trust and dramatically strengthens customer loyalty.

What material are the key fobs made of?

We exclusively use high-quality, solid stainless steel. It guarantees durability and a premium feel. Full details on finishes (polished/matte) and custom shapes are available at Materials & Craftsmanship.

What's the difference between the classic KeyRefinder® and the KeyShopper®?

The classic KeyRefinder® focuses purely on the key return system in elegant, compact forms. The KeyShopper® is a 2-in-1 solution: it combines the return service with a built-in shopping cart token – an everyday companion that will never be removed from the key ring.

Is the return service free for life?

Transparency matters to us: unrealistic promises like a "free-for-life service" are not part of our business philosophy. We prioritize quality, reliable Swiss service, and responsible data handling – with fair, transparent subscription terms for full data privacy.
